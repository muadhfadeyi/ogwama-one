How It All Connects

Four simple pieces. One smooth flow.

Instead of separate spreadsheets, paper PDFs, and delayed bank transfers, every part of OGWAMA talks to the next automatically.

01 • 2027 Online Licensing

Operators register online & get a QR pass

PSPs and ₦200k Waste Vendors upload their CAC, 3-year Ogun tax clearance, and truck proof online. Each political ward locks to one PSP only, and approved operators receive a scannable digital QR pass.

02 • Clear Digital Bills

Factories, plazas & estates get one simple bill

Every company, shop, or organised estate/CDC is linked to its exact ward PSP. Estates can count housing units right on the platform so bulk bills are fair, clear, and easy to pay online or at any bank.

03 • Instant Split Payments

Money splits automatically the moment it is paid

When a factory pays, 60% goes straight to the PSP and 40% goes straight to OGWAMA (and 80% / 20% for estates). Funds never get stuck waiting for approval letters—so PSPs stay funded and trucks keep moving.

04 • Live Oversight & Clean Recycling

3-second QR checks & real-time visibility

Factory gates scan a vendor's QR pass to stop unlicensed buyers and HSE double-crossing. Residents find their ward PSP on a simple map, report missed pickups in one tap, and every OGWAMA department sees live reports automatically.
