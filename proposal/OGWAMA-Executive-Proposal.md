# OGWAMA One — A Simple Digital Proposal for OGWAMA

**Prepared For:** Ogun State Waste Management Authority (OGWAMA)  
**Presented By:** Muadh Fadeyi / Team 1

**One simple system so waste collection, instant PSP payouts, and 2027 online licensing all work together—at ₦0 upfront cost to Ogun State.**

---

## How Everything Works Together (4 Simple Steps)

1. **2027 Online Licensing & QR Passes**
   PSPs and ₦200k Waste Vendors apply and renew online instead of printing PDFs. Each political ward locks to **one PSP only**, and every approved operator gets a scannable digital **QR Pass**.

2. **Clear Digital Bills**
   Factories, commercial centers, and organised estates/CDCs get one clear digital bill linked to their ward PSP. Estates can verify housing unit counts right on the platform so bulk bills are fair and easy to pay.

3. **Instant Split Payments**
   The moment a bill is paid, the payment gateway splits funds automatically—**60% to the PSP & 40% to OGWAMA** for industrial/commercial bills, and **80% / 20%** for estates. No claim letters, no waiting months behind manual approvals.

4. **3-Second QR Checks & Live Visibility**
   Factory gates scan QR passes in 3 seconds to stop unlicensed buyers and HSE double-crossing. Residents find their ward PSP on a simple map and report missed pickups in one tap, while every OGWAMA department sees live numbers automatically.

---

## Why Everyone Wins

- **OGWAMA:** Live visibility across Finance, Operations, Commercial, Recycling & PRS, higher capture, and zero paper bottlenecks.
- **PSP Operators:** Paid in seconds instead of months, with protected non-overlapping political wards.
- **Licensed Waste Vendors:** Protected access to assigned factories with instant QR verification.
- **Residents & Companies:** Transparent billing, known ward PSPs, and easy complaint reporting.

---

## Cost to Government: ₦0 Upfront

We build, host, and run the platform at **₦0 upfront cost to OGWAMA or the Ogun State Government**. The platform is funded solely through a small convenience fee at checkout (**1.5%–2%** on digital bills and **₦7,500** on online license renewals), leaving **100% of OGWAMA’s statutory share untouched**.

1. **Weeks 1–4:** Launch 2027 Online Licensing & QR Passes.
2. **Weeks 5–8:** Turn on Instant 60/40 & 80/20 Split Payments in Abeokuta.
